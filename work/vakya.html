The problem

Voice agents fail at the first turn. 55 percent of 455 teams abandoned theirs, and open-vocabulary speech recognition errs by 50 percent or more on Indian field speech. The failure lives between what was heard and what was executed.

What I built

Move the constraint from the model to the decoder. A byte-level grammar compiled from the target's own schema constrains every decoded token, so the action is well-formed by construction in any script. The grammar also carries date formats, numeral systems, currency units and enum members, which makes it the localisation layer too.

What proves it

A garbled utterance can produce a valid action or a refusal. Guardrail, audit trail and refusal stop being promises and become architecture. The target is a five-dollar device, offline, with no task-specific weights.
