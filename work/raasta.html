The problem

A route planner that assumes one body is a route planner for nobody. A 150 mm kerb, a parked cart, a broken slab: the same street is passable for one person and closed for another. Most maps answer with distance and call it done.

What I built

A photo goes through twelve stages: two detectors, agreement, a footpath mask, depth, measurement, placement, impact, a vision model, a reasoner, a receipt. Seventy-four body profiles across seven categories change the cost of the same kerb. Each segment is known_barrier, known_clear with a date, osm_tag, or unknown. No recent image means unknown, never safe.

What proves it

On the Chandigarh demo the shortest route is 622 m and meets 33 placed barriers. The RAASTA route is 1.04 km and meets none. A full scan runs in about 26 seconds on a 4 GB laptop GPU; the detectors alone take about 6.
