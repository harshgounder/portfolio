The problem

Tool lists lie. A folder of 300 links says nothing about what you can actually run, what it covers, and what you have never touched. Coverage gets asserted instead of measured.

What I built

A manifest of 64 verified tools, 28 authored domains plus 9 pointers, and a 1,000-factor denominator across sixteen lenses. Every factor carries a coverage record. Every excluded entry carries a reason. A gate and a claims ledger keep the scoreboard honest.

What proves it

Coverage is 39.7 percent of the denominator, against a measured ceiling of 95.0 percent, with 6 of 1,000 factors observed. The defects found in the project are listed in the repo, including the ones in its own scoring.
